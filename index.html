Honest answers

The questions everyone asks.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Modern voices are natural, and the agent says it is an assistant. Customers care about being helped in two seconds, not about who picked up.

What happens with hard questions?

The assistant passes the conversation to your team with a full summary. It never guesses on things it does not know.

What about our data?

It stays yours. Your documents and customer data live in a private setup and never train public models.

Which languages?

Ten, in the same assistant: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Ukrainian, Turkish, Portuguese and Russian. It detects the customer's language and answers in it - even if your business works in one.

We are not a tech company. Will we manage?

That is exactly who this is built for. Setup is a guided flow - paste a link, answer a few questions, done. No code, no IT project, and support is one message away.

Will it replace my people?

No. It takes the routine - the same ten questions, the missed calls, the paperwork - so your people do the work only people can do.

What does it cost?

Plans start at €79 a month. No setup fees, cancel monthly, 30-day money-back guarantee. All prices are on the plans page - nothing hidden behind a call.

Talk to Mina today - the live demo takes minutes. Your own assistant can be answering real customers the same day.
