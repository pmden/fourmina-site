Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fourmina handles personal data under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR. It covers our website, our own business records, and the data that flows through the assistants we run for our customers.

1. Who we are

KROK GROUP LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, Company No. 17183343, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom, operating the Fourmina service. Contact for all privacy matters: hello@fourmina.com.

2. Our two roles

We act in two distinct roles:

  • Processor. When a business uses a Fourmina assistant, the calls, call recordings, transcripts, chat logs and order details of that business's end customers are processed by us on behalf of and on the instructions of that business. The business is the data controller; we are the processor under our Data Processing Addendum.
  • Controller. For our own operations - customer accounts, billing, contracts, enquiries, demo interactions and website analytics - we are the data controller.

If you are an end customer of a business that uses Fourmina and you want to exercise your data rights over a call or chat, the business you spoke with is the controller. Contact them first; we will assist them in responding. You can also write to us and we will forward your request.

3. Data we process as controller

  • Account and contact data - name, business name, email, phone, role. Purpose: providing the Service, support, communication. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or steps before a contract.
  • Billing data - plan, invoices, payment status. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers. Purpose: charging for the Service and accounting. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Demo interactions - if you call our demo line or chat with our demo assistant, the interaction is recorded and transcribed to show the product and improve the demo. The assistant tells you at the start that you are talking to an AI. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) or consent where required.
  • Enquiries - messages you send us. Legal basis: legitimate interests or steps before a contract.
  • Website data - aggregate visit statistics and the theme/language preferences stored in your browser. No advertising profiles, no sale of data. Legal basis: legitimate interests; consent where required for non-essential storage.

4. Data we process as processor

For each customer's assistant we process, on the customer's documented instructions:

  • call audio and call recordings;
  • transcripts of calls;
  • chat logs from WhatsApp and website chat;
  • booking and order details the end customer provides during the conversation (for example a name, phone number, table booking or food order).

We use this data only to run the customer's assistant and to provide the customer with their conversation history and analytics. We do not use it for our own purposes and we do not use it to train public AI models. The terms of this processing are set out in the Data Processing Addendum.

5. Calls and chat processing

Conversations with a Fourmina assistant - calls and chats - are transcribed and processed to deliver the service: to understand the request, answer it, and hand the business a record of what was said. This is how the product works, and it is disclosed, not hidden:

  • The assistant says it is an AI. At the start of every call and every chat the assistant identifies itself as an AI assistant, as required by Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the EU AI Act). Businesses using Fourmina cannot switch this disclosure off.
  • No covert recording. We do not record or transcribe conversations covertly. Processing happens within the disclosed interaction with the assistant, and the business you talk to is responsible for any further notice its local law requires.
  • Retention. Transcripts and recordings are kept for the duration of the customer relationship at most, within the retention periods in section 6 below; the business can configure a shorter period or instruct deletion at any time.
  • Who is responsible. The business you talk to is the controller of its end-customer conversation data. Fourmina processes it on that business's behalf and on its instructions, under the Data Processing Addendum.

6. How long we keep data

  • Transcripts, recordings and chat logs (processor role): retained per the customer's instruction. Default retention is 12 months, after which they are deleted. Customers can choose a shorter or longer period in their settings or instruct deletion at any time.
  • Account and billing data: for the duration of the contract, then as long as tax and company law require.
  • Demo recordings: up to 90 days.
  • Enquiries: up to 24 months after the last exchange.

7. Who helps us process data (subprocessors)

We use a small set of vetted service providers, each under a data processing agreement:

  • a cloud hosting provider with hosting in the EU;
  • LLM API providers for language understanding and generation;
  • a telephony provider for carrying and connecting calls;
  • a payment processor for subscription billing;
  • an email provider for transactional email.

The current named list of subprocessors for your engagement is available on request at hello@fourmina.com. Customers are notified of subprocessor changes as described in the DPA.

8. International transfers

Where a provider processes personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on recognised safeguards: an adequacy decision where one exists, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, supplemented by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for transfers subject to UK GDPR. Copies of the relevant safeguards are available on request.

9. EU representative

KROK GROUP LTD is established in the United Kingdom. Where we offer services to businesses in the European Union and process personal data of individuals in the EU, Article 27 EU GDPR requires us to designate a representative in the Union. The representative's details will be published here once appointed; until then, all enquiries can be addressed to hello@fourmina.com.

10. Security

Personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to people who need it to do their job, protected by access controls and logged. We review access regularly. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify affected customers and, where required, the supervisory authority without undue delay.

11. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to hello@fourmina.com; we respond within one month.

You can complain to a supervisory authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the EU, the data protection authority of your country. We would rather fix the problem first, so please contact us before escalating.

12. Automated decision-making

Fourmina assistants hold conversations automatically, but they do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about individuals. Conversations that need a real decision are escalated to a human at the business you contacted.

13. What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not run advertising profiles. We do not use customer data or end-customer conversations to train public AI models.

14. Changes

We update this policy when our practices change. Material changes are announced to customers by email or in the product. The date above shows the current version.
